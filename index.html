Built around how people actually look things up.

No account walls, no ads, no scrolling through irrelevant results — just the numbers your area actually needs, kept current by the people who use them.

🔎

Search and filter, bilingually

Look things up by business type, city, or street — type a few letters in Tamil or English and matching results narrow instantly.

☎

Call or WhatsApp in one tap

Every listing has a direct call button and a WhatsApp button. No copying numbers, no switching between apps to find who you meant to contact.

✎

Add a business yourself

Know a shop or service that isn't listed? Submit it directly from the app — name, type, city, street, and contact numbers — for an admin to review.

⚑

Something wrong? Flag it

Report outdated or incorrect listings, or message the admin team directly if you need help — every report goes straight to someone who can act on it.

🔔

Live notifications

Get notified the moment there's a new announcement or a reply to something you sent in — turn it on once in Settings and it runs quietly in the background.

✓

Checked before it's public

Every new listing is reviewed by a community admin before it goes live, so the directory stays accurate instead of turning into an open wiki free-for-all.

Everyone starts as a browser. Some go further.

AdiraiDials doesn't split into separate apps for different people — it's one app that opens up depending on what you need to do.

No account needed

Browse & call

Open the app and start searching straight away. Every listing, every call and WhatsApp button, works without signing up for anything.

Sign up in the app

Contribute

Submit new businesses, report bad listings, or message the admins. Your submissions are reviewed before they go live, and you'll be notified when an admin replies.

Community moderators

Administer

Admins review submissions and reports, manage password resets, post announcements, and keep the directory's cities, streets, and business types organized.
